vibe-co

A directory of small games built from the vibe-co templates.

  • auto-battles
    Asynchronous auto-battler — build a team once, then watch it fight five opponents pulled from recent runs.
  • coin-flip
    Coin flipping game.
  • mapoguesser
    Guess the location from a 3D world view.
  • sky-strike
    Aerial action game.
  • osm_globe
    Spin a 3D globe of OpenStreetMap vector data with Google Maps-style pan and zoom.
  • snake
    Classic snake.
